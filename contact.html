Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Quick answers before visiting our New Town dessert kiosk.

Are all waffles really 100% pure vegetarian?

Yes! Our entire batter recipe is completely eggless, prepared using fresh milk and pure ingredients. We never handle or process eggs anywhere on our premises.

Where exactly inside Astra Tower are you located?

We are on the Ground Floor at Kiosk ANRK-002, just steps from the central entrance atrium. Look for our warm lit signboard and aroma of fresh Belgian waffles!

Can I get waffles packed for takeaway?

Absolutely. We serve takeaways in custom ventilated rigid boxes that allow steam to escape while retaining heat, so your waffles stay crispy until you get home.

How early should I book for festival or bulk event catering?

For live event stations or college festivals, we recommend reaching out 3 to 7 days in advance. For office bulk orders (20+ waffles), 24 hours advance notice is ideal.
