Our Home At Astra Tower

Kiosk ANRK-002: Built On Warmth & Craft

When you walk into the ground floor arcade of Astra Tower in New Town, the unmistakable aroma of caramelized sugar and warm vanilla leads you directly to W4 WAFFLE. Handcrafted with warm natural timber, rustic jute rope filament lamps, and spotless stainless steel baking counters, our kiosk was designed as an intimate culinary sanctuary.

Here, every waffle is treated not as fast food, but as a culinary canvas. Patrons gather around our counter to watch batter hit the sizzling irons, hear the golden sizzle, and witness the artistic drizzle of warm Belgian chocolate.

Our Culinary Pillars

The Four Pillars Of W4 Craftsmanship

We hold ourselves to uncompromising standards of vegetarian integrity, ingredient purity, and artisanal technique.

🥬

100% Pure Vegetarian

Every single batter, ganache, cream cheese drizzle, and topping is strictly 100% pure vegetarian. Zero eggs, zero animal gelatin, zero cross-contamination.

🧇

Cast-Iron Precision

We bake exclusively on high-thermal-density cast-iron Belgian griddles calibrated to precisely 210°C, ensuring a deeply crisp golden exterior with a cloud-soft, steaming interior.

🍫

Authentic Chocolate Sourcing

We refuse synthetic chocolate compounds. We source genuine Belgian cocoa butter ganaches, authentic Italian Nutella, and genuine KitKat wafers for authentic depth of flavor.

✨

Baked Fresh to Order

No reheating, no pre-made soggy shells. Your waffle batter is poured fresh only after you order, served scalding warm in custom breathable parchment sleeves.

The Artisan Process

From Golden Batter To Final Drizzle

Our batter is aerated daily using premium wheat flour, farm-fresh milk, and pure vanilla bean essence. When poured onto the griddle, the heat locks in moisture while crystallizing surface sugars for that signature crunch Kolkata food lovers rave about.
